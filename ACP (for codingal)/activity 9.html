CSS Effects

CSS effects are many types. For example we have: background effects and we also have gradient effects. The background behind the texts is a Background Image..

Background Effects..

Background effects can be two types. One is Background image and the other is Background Gradients aka the Gradient Effects. The gradients are two types. One is Linear Gradient and the other is Radiant Gradient. today we will use these two. The background behind the texts are Linear Gradient.. Linear Gradient basically starts from top left, top right, bottom left and bottom right. Lets move onto Radient Gradient.

Background Effects

Now that we have done Linear Gradients, lets do Radient Gradient. Radient Gradient basically starts from the middle while Linear Gradient starts from top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right etc. The background behind the texts are Radient Gradient..
